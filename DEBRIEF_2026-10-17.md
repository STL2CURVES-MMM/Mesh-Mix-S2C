## Mount: evidence gathered

`why_demoted2.py` was run on
`test files/owner problem files/sharps_mount_PETG_rev8.stl` with tolerance
0.06917 and quality 0.7.  The first CAD build had eight invalid faces and no
failed face constructions.  Important invalid regions were:

- 16: cylinder, 759 triangles;
- 36: torus, 1,740 triangles;
- 198: cylinder, 305 triangles; and
- 199: torus, 3,448 triangles.

Saved first-build faces were inspected with `face_status.py`,
`edge_status.py`, and `wire_cross.py`:

- cylinder 16 has a self-intersecting wire.  Its UV boundary contains a
  roughly 0.3 mm in-and-back tongue; sampled edges 3--6 cross twice;
- cylinder 198 has `InvalidImbricationOfWires` and an outer-wire crossing at
  its boundary;
- torus 36 has `InvalidImbricationOfWires`; and
- torus 199 has `BadOrientationOfSubshape`.

This confirms the patchwork's cause: invalid analytic faces are demoted to
freeform cells, and later failures fall back to mesh facets.  It is not a
general fitting-accuracy problem.

### Rejected experiment

An unconditional local border-tongue rule was tried.  It used mesh adjacency,
corner contact with a neighbouring analytic surface, normal/distance
acceptance, and a strictly better surface fit.  On the mount it did not remove
cylinder 16's spike and increased the first-build invalid-face count from 8 to
9.  It was removed immediately and is not in the current code.

### Current WIP implementation (not validated)

`engine/mmm/cad.py` now has a more conservative candidate repair:

1. Build the normal analytic CAD faces first.
2. Ask OpenCASCADE which region faces contain a genuinely self-intersecting
   wire.
3. Only for those source regions, consider border-strip triangles that fit a
   neighbouring analytic/swept surface better and whose three vertices already
   touch that neighbour.
4. Rebuild the trial result; retain it only if self-crossing wires decrease
   **and** total invalid faces do not increase.  Otherwise restore the original
   segmentation.

This scope is geometric/topological rather than model-specific, but the mount
diagnostic was interrupted before it returned a result.  No claim of a fix,
suite safety, or output improvement can be made yet.
